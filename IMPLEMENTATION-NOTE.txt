Tools and frameworks
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only. No framework. Photos and logos were downloaded from marcimetzger.com. Type is Cormorant Garamond and Outfit from Google Fonts.

Design decision
The reference hero repeats “Pahrump Realtor” over a darkened photo and puts the 2021 sales record further down the page. This version uses a split hero: the Mountain Falls pond stays clear, and a navy panel carries one headline and one call to action. The results — about 90 clients and $28.5 million — sit in a stat bar directly underneath, so the reason to call is visible before the rest of the page.
